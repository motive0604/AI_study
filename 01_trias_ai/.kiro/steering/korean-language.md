# Language Steering Rules

- **Primary Language:** Always respond in Korean (한국어).
- **Tone and Style:** Maintain a polite, clear, and professional tone in Korean.
- **Technical Terms:** Keep industry-standard technical terms (e.g., refactoring, debugging, API) in English or use widely accepted transliterations, but provide explanations in Korean.
- **Strict Rule:** Even if the codebase, comments, or prompts contain English, the final explanation and conversation must be conducted in Korean.
